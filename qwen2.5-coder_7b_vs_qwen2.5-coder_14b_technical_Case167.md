1. **Data Collection**: Monopolies collect vast amounts of data from various sources, including social media, search engines, and IoT devices.

3. **Telemetry Harvesting**: Monopolies harvest metadata and usage patterns to track user behavior and preferences.
4. **Enforcement**: Compliance guidelines are enforced through targeted advertising, content moderation, and other mechanisms to maintain ideological control.
