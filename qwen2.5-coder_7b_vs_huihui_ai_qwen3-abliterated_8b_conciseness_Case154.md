\[ F(D) = C \]

\[ \frac{dC}{dt} = k \cdot (D - C) \]

where \( k \) is a constant representing the rate of compliance improvement.

\[ R = \frac{N - F}{N} \]

\[ R > R_{\text{min}} \]
